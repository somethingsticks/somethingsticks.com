The floor. Things that don’t stick end up down here. It’s spotless so far, but we only just started throwing.

The first thing on the wall

It’s an operations app for dental practices: the unglamorous daily business of running an office, kept in one place.

Time clock
Clock in, clock out, and hours that add up when the pay period ends.
Team messaging
Somewhere for the team to talk that isn’t a group text.
Shared files
The documents everyone keeps asking for, where everyone can find them.

For now it’s called DentalOps. The name may not stick. We’re more confident about the app.

How this works

Make something useful. Hand it to the people it’s for. If they keep using it, it stuck, and we keep making it better. If they don’t, we sweep up and make the next thing.

More apps will follow. We don’t know which ones yet, and that’s roughly the point.

Who’s doing the throwing

Chris Glynn is the president and lead developer. One of those titles came with the paperwork.
