Privacy

Last updated November 15, 2026.

Who this is

Something Sticks, LLC is a software company in Iowa. This page covers this website, somethingsticks.com, and the apps we make. “We” means Something Sticks, LLC.

This website

This site has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising. The fonts and images are served from this site, so your browser isn’t sent anywhere else to load the page.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the host’s servers receive your IP address and basic request details when you load a page, and GitHub may keep those in its logs for security and operations. We don’t receive those logs. GitHub’s own privacy statement describes how it handles them.

When you email us

If you write to us, we get your email address and whatever you put in the message. We use that to answer you and to fix what you wrote about. We keep the conversation for as long as it’s useful for that, and you can ask us to delete it.

Our apps

Our first app is in development and has not been released. Before it is, this section will list what the app collects, why, and who can see it.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell personal information.
  • We don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We don’t share your information with anyone except the service providers that help us operate, or when the law requires it.

Asking about your information

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by emailing support@somethingsticks.com.

Changes

If what we collect or do changes, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
