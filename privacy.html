Privacy, in plain words

Last updated November 15, 2026.

Who this is

Something Sticks, LLC is a software company in West Des Moines, Iowa. This page covers this website, somethingsticks.com, and the apps we make. “We” means Something Sticks, LLC.

This website

This site has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising. The fonts and images are served from this site, so your browser isn’t sent anywhere else to load the page.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the host’s servers receive your IP address and basic request details when you load a page, and GitHub may keep those in its logs for security and operations. We don’t receive those logs. GitHub’s own privacy statement describes how it handles them.

When you email us

If you write to us, we get your email address and whatever you put in the message. We use that to answer you and to fix what you wrote about. We keep the conversation for as long as it’s useful for that, and you can ask us to delete it.

Our apps

Our first app is an operations app for dental practices, currently called DentalOps. A practice sets it up for its team, and the team uses it for a time clock, team messaging, and shared files. To do those jobs, the app stores:

  • Account details, such as your name, work role, and the email address you sign in with.
  • Time clock records, meaning when you clock in and out.
  • Messages you send and receive in the app.
  • Files that you or your practice upload.
  • Basic technical information needed to run the service and keep it secure, such as device type and sign-in times.

That information belongs to the practice and its team. We use it to run the app for them, and for nothing else.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell personal information.
  • We don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We don’t share your information with anyone except the service providers that run the app for us, described below, or when the law requires it.

Who helps us run things

App data is stored with Google Cloud (Firebase), which hosts it on our behalf. This website is hosted by GitHub. If we add another provider that handles personal information, we’ll list it here.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep app data for as long as a practice uses the app. You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and data by emailing support@somethingsticks.com. Some records, such as time clock entries, are your workplace’s business records, so we may need to involve your practice before changing or deleting them.

Children

Our apps are workplace tools. They aren’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If what we collect or do changes, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
