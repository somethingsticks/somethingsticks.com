Something came unstuck?

Support for apps made by Something Sticks, LLC, including DentalOps.

Email us

support@somethingsticks.com

That address goes to a person, not a ticket robot. We answer as soon as we can.

What helps us help you

  • Which app you’re using, and the name of your practice or workplace if you use it through one.
  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead.
  • The device you’re on, such as an iPhone, an iPad, or a web browser.
  • A screenshot, if there’s something worth seeing. Crop out anything private first.

Please don’t send passwords or patient information by email. We will never ask for either.

Signing in

If your workplace set up your account, an administrator there can usually sort out sign-in trouble fastest. If that doesn’t work, write to us and we’ll look into it.

Your data

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have your account and data deleted, email support@somethingsticks.com from the address on your account. The privacy page explains what we collect and why.

Everything else

For anything that isn’t a support question, hello@somethingsticks.com is the place.
