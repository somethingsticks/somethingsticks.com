Support

Help with apps made by Something Sticks, LLC.

Email us

support@somethingsticks.com

Messages go to a person, and we answer as soon as we can.

What to include

  • Which app you’re using, and the name of your workplace if you use it through one.
  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead.
  • The device you’re on, such as an iPhone, an iPad, or a web browser.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Crop out anything private first.

Please don’t send passwords or other sensitive information by email. We will never ask for them.

Signing in

If your workplace set up your account, an administrator there can usually resolve sign-in problems fastest. If that doesn’t work, write to us.

Your data

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have your account and data deleted, email support@somethingsticks.com from the address on your account. The privacy page explains what we collect and why.

Everything else

For anything that isn’t a support question, write to hello@somethingsticks.com.
